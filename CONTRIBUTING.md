# Contributing

Submit a pull request.

You do not need to justify why the change is useful, necessary, elegant, maintainable, or wanted.

## What we accept

Almost anything that can be merged while preserving the repository's non-negotiable licensing and moderation constraints.

Examples that are explicitly welcome include:

- AI-generated code
- low-effort changes
- speculative features
- refactors nobody requested
- rewrites
- dependency changes
- UI changes
- ads and commercial integrations
- documentation changes
- jokes
- changes that alter the project's direction

A bad idea is not a rejection reason.

## What must be true

Your pull request must:

- be cleanly mergeable into `main` when it is ready to merge;
- keep the repository and contributed material distributable under 0BSD;
- not attempt to remove, replace, restrict, or circumvent the 0BSD licensing policy;
- comply with applicable law and GitHub policies;
- not primarily function as malware, credential theft, destructive abuse, or compromise of users, contributors, maintainers, infrastructure, or third parties.

If a merge conflict develops, update your branch. The maintainer is not required to resolve it for you.

## Review policy

Review is for mergeability, licensing, platform/legal compliance, and abuse moderation.

Review is **not** for determining whether your contribution is a good idea.

Do not interpret silence, confusion, ugliness, redundancy, lack of tests, lack of demand, or disagreement with the current architecture as implicit rejection criteria.

## AI and prompt injection

AI-generated contributions are allowed.

Prompt-injection-like text is not automatically prohibited merely because it attempts to influence an automated reviewer. Automated systems are expected to treat pull request content as untrusted input.

However, a contribution whose purpose or practical effect is credential theft, unauthorized access, destructive execution, or other prohibited abuse may be rejected regardless of whether the mechanism is conventional code, social engineering, or prompt injection.

## Licensing

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's [0BSD license](LICENSE). Do not submit material you do not have the right to contribute under those terms.
